What It Is

Not a status page.
A look ahead.

Most transit apps tell you what already happened — service alerts after delays begin, outage notices after elevators break. TrakkRecord™ works the other way around. It reads the patterns in years of public NYC transit data and tells you what's likely to happen next.

The result is a station-level forecast you can pull up before you leave the house — fast enough to check on the way to the platform, from any phone, kiosk, or partner app.
